# T10 — External legal-resource link review

**Date:** 6 October 2026

## Scope

Rechecked the external destinations published on the Legal Resources page and revisited the links that had produced ambiguous results during the original technical audit.

## Current Legal Resources page

The current page uses these unique external destinations:

- Supreme Court of Sri Lanka — https://supremecourt.lk/
- Court of Appeal of Sri Lanka — https://courtofappeal.lk/
- Judicial Service Commission — https://jsc.gov.lk/web/
- Parliament of Sri Lanka, Acts & Bills — https://www.parliament.lk/en/business-of-parliament/acts-bills
- Department of Government Printing — https://documents.gov.lk/
- Ministry of Justice — https://www.moj.gov.lk/
- Attorney General's Department — https://agd.gov.lk/
- Department of Labour — https://labourdept.gov.lk/
- Mediation Boards Commission — https://mediation.gov.lk/en/

Each destination was opened and its visible content checked against the description used on the website. No current Legal Resources link required replacement.

## Earlier ambiguous destinations

- ICTA legal framework: currently live and contains the expected Electronic Transactions / digital-law material. The earlier 502 was not treated as a permanent broken link.
- LawNet: the root site remained unreliable in this checking environment, while specific LawNet legal materials remain discoverable. LawNet is not currently linked from the Legal Resources page, so no live-page replacement was made.
- justice.lk / older justice destination: not present in the current Legal Resources page. The current page uses the Ministry of Justice destination at moj.gov.lk.
- Facebook: browser-fetch restrictions can prevent an automated content check. It is not a legal-resource card and no T10 change was made on that basis.

## Decision

T10 is complete. No website edit was justified. The current Legal Resources page should remain unchanged.
