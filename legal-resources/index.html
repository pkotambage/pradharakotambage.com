Official sources

Legal Resources

A curated starting point for official Sri Lankan legal, judicial and public-sector information. For now, this page links only to websites maintained by courts, Parliament, government departments and official justice institutions.

Why official sources? Laws, gazettes, court information and institutional procedures can change. Where possible, it is better to begin with the institution responsible for publishing or administering the information.
