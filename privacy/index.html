Website information

Privacy

This page explains, in simple terms, how personal information is handled in connection with this website in its current form.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

1. The website in its current form

pradharakotambage.com is presently a public legal-information and legal-literacy website. It does not currently provide user accounts, online payment facilities, newsletter registration, online consultation forms or a member area.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you contact Pradhara Kotambage using the email address or telephone number published on this website, any information you provide will be received through the communication method you choose. That information may include your name, contact details and the contents of your message.

Please avoid sending confidential, privileged or highly sensitive information through ordinary email unless it is appropriate to do so. Contacting an email address or telephone number shown on this website does not, by itself, create an Attorney-at-Law/client relationship.

3. Optional website analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to understand readership and improve its information and navigation. The Google Analytics script is loaded only after you select “Allow analytics”. Declining does not affect access to the website.

Analytics may include pages viewed, visit duration, scrolls, outbound link clicks, file downloads, embedded-video engagement, referring websites, approximate location and browser or device information. Google processes connection information, including your IP address, to provide the service. Analytics data may be processed outside Sri Lanka.

Advertising features and advertising personalisation are disabled in the website tag. We do not configure Analytics to collect visitors’ names, email addresses, telephone numbers, client information or the text entered into the article search. Outbound-link and download events may include the public destination address of the link. Site-search and form-interaction measurement are disabled. The page address sent by our tag excludes query strings and fragments, and the referring address is reduced to its website origin.

Google's handling of data is explained in its Privacy Policy and information about sites using Google services. Website hosting and network providers may also process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, independently of your analytics choice.

4. Cookies and your choice

We store your analytics choice in your browser's local storage for up to 180 days. If you allow analytics, Google Analytics uses first-party cookies, including _ga and cookies beginning _ga_, to distinguish browsers and sessions. Our tag limits their lifetime to 180 days without extending the expiry on each visit.

You can use “Analytics settings” in the footer of any page to allow or decline analytics. Declining after previously allowing it disables further collection on this site, removes accessible Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. This does not erase data already received by Google. Clearing browser storage removes your saved choice. If storage is unavailable, your choice may apply only to the current page.

This website does not use advertising or remarketing cookies. External websites reached through links have their own cookie and privacy practices.

5. External links

The Legal Resources section and other pages may link to courts, Parliament, government departments and other external websites. Once you leave pradharakotambage.com, the privacy practices of the external website apply.

6. Use and retention of correspondence

Information sent directly by email or telephone may be used to respond to the communication, maintain necessary professional or administrative records, protect legitimate interests, or comply with applicable legal and professional obligations. Correspondence is not intended to be retained for longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is kept.

7. Changes to this page

This Privacy page may be revised as the website develops or if its technical features change. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.

8. Contact

Questions specifically about the privacy practices of this website may be directed to pkotambage@gmail.com.

Current position

No online accounts, payments, newsletter signup or consultation form are currently operated through this website.
