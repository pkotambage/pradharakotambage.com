Dispute Resolution

Resolving disputes constructively.

Not every dispute needs to end with a prolonged court battle.

Understanding the dispute is often the first step towards resolving it.

Dispute resolution includes a range of approaches through which parties may attempt to resolve disagreements more efficiently, practically and constructively. Depending on the circumstances, these may include negotiation, settlement, mediation and arbitration.

My professional interest is increasingly focused on these methods of resolving disputes, particularly mediation and other consensual approaches that allow parties to identify their real concerns, explore practical solutions and, where possible, reach agreements without unnecessary conflict.
