Dispute Resolution

Resolving disputes constructively.

Not every dispute needs to end with a prolonged court battle.

Understanding the dispute is often the first step towards resolving it.

Disputes may be addressed in different ways depending on their nature, the parties' objectives and the applicable law.

This section begins with a connected series of practical guides on mediation: what it is, how to prepare for it, and the new legal framework introduced in Sri Lanka in 2026.

Ways disputes may be resolved

Negotiation — the parties communicate directly to try to reach an agreement.

Settlement — the parties agree on terms that bring the dispute to an end.

Mediation — a neutral mediator helps the parties communicate and explore a mutually acceptable resolution.

Arbitration — an arbitrator considers the dispute and makes a decision, usually under an arbitration agreement.
