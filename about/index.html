About me

I am Pradhara Kotambage, an Attorney-at-Law in Sri Lanka. My professional work has principally involved civil and commercial disputes, litigation and court practice.

My academic background includes a B.Sc. in Physical Science from the University of Colombo, followed by legal studies at Sri Lanka Law College. I was admitted as an Attorney-at-Law in Sri Lanka in January 2017.

Years of working with legal disputes have also strengthened my interest in how disagreements can be understood, managed and resolved before they become unnecessarily prolonged. Alongside litigation, my professional interests increasingly include negotiation, settlement, mediation and arbitration.

Why this website exists

People rarely experience the law as neatly separated subjects such as civil procedure, labour law or the law of obligations. A legal problem usually begins in a much more ordinary way: a letter arrives, money is not paid, employment ends unexpectedly, a disagreement grows, or a court case has begun and the process is difficult to understand.

The purpose of this website is to make Sri Lankan law easier to understand in those real-life situations. The Legal Guides are therefore organised around practical questions, with an emphasis on explaining what the law generally means, what usually happens in practice, and what distinctions may matter.

Law, practice and dispute resolution

The website also looks beyond legal rules alone. Law in Practice | නීතිය ප්‍රායෝගිකව is intended to explore how law operates in actual situations, including the practical realities that may not always be obvious from reading legislation or a textbook.

The Dispute Resolution section develops a related theme: not every disagreement is best understood only in terms of winning and losing a case. Negotiation, settlement, mediation and arbitration each have different purposes and limitations. For now, these pages are primarily educational, and the section will develop gradually alongside my continuing professional work and study in dispute resolution.
